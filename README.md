I am a Security GIS Specialist and Full-Stack GIS Developer focused on building operational geospatial systems that connect maps, field information, resources, databases, real-time data, and decision-support workflows.

Since 2021, I have worked on GIS solutions supporting operational requirements within the Palestinian Police, including major national operations, operational planning, spatial analysis, security assessment systems, interactive operational maps, and command dashboards.

My development experience extends beyond traditional GIS into full-stack applications, real-time tracking platforms, APIs, spatial databases, mobile/web systems, and operational management platforms.

My current research direction is GeoCommand AI, exploring how Artificial Intelligence can enhance operational GIS through dynamic risk assessment, predictive early warning, intelligent dispatch, resource optimization, and explainable Human-in-the-Loop decision support.
